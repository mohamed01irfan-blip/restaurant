🌿 Our Story

A Legacy of Pure Vegetarian Cuisine

Founded in 2009, Pasumai Family Restaurant was born from a simple vision — to serve wholesome, delicious vegetarian meals that bring families together. The name "Pasumai" (பசுமை) means "greenery" in Tamil, reflecting our commitment to fresh, plant-based ingredients.

Every dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients, traditional spices, and the kind of love that only a family kitchen can offer. From crispy dosas to hearty thalis, we celebrate the richness of Indian vegetarian cuisine.
